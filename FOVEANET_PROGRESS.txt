A1. FRAME LENGTH                                    notebook 03, section 8
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The sweep that chose 50 ms was obsolete: it fitted a fresh mixture per frame and
selected by size, so it said nothing about a model that carries the mixture
forward and selects by persistence. Redone properly, with dwell in seconds and
jitter in pixels per millisecond, since windows of different lengths cannot
otherwise be compared at all.

A1.1  FRAME LENGTH IS SET BY THE MODEL, NOT CHOSEN FREELY. Three limits bracket
      it, two of them properties of the model rather than the data:
        - the fit needs events. At 1 ms a frame holds about 43 events, roughly
          14 per component, and concentration collapses from 18.5 at 5 ms to
          5.1 at 1 ms.
        - the compute must fit inside the window. A warm fit costs about 1 ms,
          so at a 1 ms window the real-time ratio is 0.9 and the system is
          already behind. A cold fit falls behind below 10 ms.
        - long windows blur motion. Concentration falls from 29.0 at 10 ms to
          22.4 at 100 ms as the component stretches over the swept path.

A1.2  SHORT FRAMES EXIST ONLY BECAUSE THE MIXTURE IS WARM-STARTED.

        window   warm dwell   cold dwell   cold frames dropped
          1 ms      0.43 s       0.00 s          45%
          5 ms      0.23 s       0.01 s           4%
         20 ms      0.81 s       0.04 s           0%
        100 ms      1.27 s       0.17 s           0%

      The advantage widens as the window shrinks: about 7x at 100 ms, 20x at
      5 to 20 ms, unbounded at 1 and 2 ms where cold dwell is zero. At 1 ms a
      per-frame fit changes fovea identity 563 times a second.

A1.3  Two quality measures disagree about the optimum, which is why the notebook
      reports a range rather than a number. Concentration peaks at 10 ms; dwell
      rises to 75 ms. Short window, tight box on a wandering fovea; long window,
      steady fovea in a loose box.

A1.4  The usable range is 10 to 50 ms. Which end to pick was left to a task
      measurement, and A4 settled it at 10 ms.

A1.5  Confirmed independently on POKER-DVS, where the subject moves about ten
      times faster. At 10 ms the fovea holds 0.23 s and switches on 4 percent of
      frames; at 33 and 50 ms it switches on about a quarter. The window should
      follow the speed of the subject rather than being a fixed constant.

A2. VOT EVENT-BASED TRACKING                        notebook 04, sections 2-4
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Downloaded from Zenodo, MD5 verified: 60 VOT Challenge 2015 sequences and 67
TrackingDataset sequences, both with per-frame ground-truth boxes already mapped
into sensor coordinates. These are the first ground truth the project has had,
which is also what made A4 possible.

A2.1  THE MODEL DOES NOT TRACK THESE SEQUENCES.

        dataset            events in GT   chosen IoU   oracle IoU   succ>0.5
        VOT (10 seqs)            6%          0.023        0.084        0%
        TrackingDataset (10)    12%          0.054        0.133        1%

A2.2  THE ORACLE COLUMN IS WHAT MAKES THIS CONCLUSIVE. Oracle IoU scores the
      best of all k components rather than the one the rule chose. At about 0.1
      the mixture never places any component on the annotated target, so no
      selection rule, no value of k and no amount of hysteresis could recover
      it. This is not a tuning failure.

A2.3  Two causes, both properties of how the benchmark was built.
        - The recordings are videos played on a monitor and filmed with a DAVIS
          camera, so the entire frame emits events continuously. NOTE, CORRECTED
          IN A10.4: this was first written as "the 60 Hz refresh dominates the
          recordings". Measured properly the refresh modulation is only 1.13 to
          1.78 times the mean, and annotated targets are usually MORE event-dense
          than the scene average. The refresh is real but secondary; the dominant
          cause is that VOT sequences contain moving backgrounds and camera
          motion by design.
        - VOT is a supervised single-object benchmark where the target is
          whatever a human annotated. The fovea is unsupervised and bottom-up.
          Scoring one against the other measures the mismatch as much as the
          method.

A2.4  The obvious remedy was tried and ruled out. Tonic's Denoise transform
      removes 45 percent of the events and leaves the overlap slightly worse,
      because it discards spatially isolated events while a display refresh is
      correlated across the whole frame. Fixing these recordings needs a
      temporal filter for the 30 and 60 Hz components, a separate piece of work.

A2.5  The fair VOT protocol was also tried: initialise from the first
      ground-truth box, as every VOT tracker does. Mean overlap rose from 0.042
      to 0.069, still far from usable, so it does not change the conclusion.

A3. POKER-DVS AND THE CARDS                         notebook 04, sections 5-7
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A3.1  TONIC'S POKER-DVS IS BOTH DEAD AND THE WRONG VERSION. Its download host no
      longer resolves, and what it would have provided is the 131 pip symbols
      already tracked and cropped to 32x32 by the authors' Matlab scripts, so
      the tracking problem is solved before the data is handed over. The
      original 128x128 recordings were fetched from the IMSE-CNM lab page
      instead, and a reader for jAER AEDAT-2.0 written for them.

A3.2  It is the right kind of data: a real deck riffled in front of a real
      sensor, no screen involved. Its spectrum peaks at 6.8 Hz with nothing near
      60 Hz, the direct contrast with A2.3, confirming that artifact belongs to
      how the benchmark was made rather than to event cameras.

A3.3  THE FOVEA CAPTURES CARDS, NOT PIPS. The fovea lands on each card as it
      crosses, but the box spans a whole card or several pips rather than one
      symbol. Capture divided by box fraction is about 2 here against roughly 18
      on gestures.

A3.4  Raising k does not fix it, and not tidily: the box covers 19 percent of
      the sensor at k=2, 6 percent at k=5, 34 percent at k=12. The k=5 row is
      also where the mixture becomes unreliable, producing a usable box on only
      61 frames against 113 to 147 elsewhere, so its tight box comes from
      discarding half the recording. No value of k isolates a pip while staying
      stable.

A3.5  The reason is that a card is not a compact blob of activity. It is a large
      flat surface whose edges and printed symbols all fire as it sweeps past,
      so the densest persistent region is the card itself.

A4. EVALUATING THE MODEL                            notebook 05
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The strongest result the project has produced, and stronger than expected.

Every earlier measurement described how the fovea behaves. None said whether the
region it keeps is the useful one, and none compared it against the obvious
alternative of cropping the middle of the sensor and hoping. This does both, by
reducing all 1077 recordings five ways and training the same classifier on each.

A4.1  DESIGN. Five conditions, each an identical (8 bins, 2 polarities, 32, 32)
      tensor, so no condition is given more numbers than another:
        full          whole sensor, downsampled, the no-reduction ceiling
        persistence   crop follows the fovea, accumulated persistence rule
        persdens      crop follows the fovea, persistence per unit area
        centre        fixed central box, same median size as the fovea box
        random        same-size box in a fixed random place
      Split by subject, holding out 6 of 23 people, because mixing a person's
      recordings across train and test lets the network recognise the person
      rather than the gesture. Network deliberately small, 71k parameters: this
      is a test of the input, not a bid for a good classifier.

A4.2  RESULTS.

        condition       50 ms    10 ms   change
        full            0.701    0.709   +0.008
        persistence     0.686    0.731   +0.045
        persdens        0.688    0.708   +0.020
        centre          0.354    0.366   +0.012
        random          0.250    0.229   -0.021

      Chance is 0.091. Median fovea box is 22 x 17 px, 2.3 percent of the
      sensor, for the persistence rule.

A4.3  WITHDRAWN. This section previously read "at 10 ms the fovea beats the
      entire sensor", on the strength of 8 seeds. It was measured on a test set
      carved out of the training subjects. On the dataset's official test
      subjects the effect disappears (see A9). The measurement below is left in
      place because the withdrawal is the point.

      On the improvised test set, 8 seeds:

        persistence  0.7314  sd 0.0081
        full         0.7091  sd 0.0123
        persdens     0.7082  sd 0.0110

        persistence - full      +0.0223   4.3 standard errors
        persistence - persdens  +0.0232   4.8 standard errors
        persdens    - full      -0.0009   0.2 standard errors

      Every one of the eight fovea runs landed above the full-frame mean, which
      is why this looked solid. Seed stability turned out to say nothing about
      stability under a change of evaluation data.

A4.4  Interpretation, stated as interpretation rather than measurement: the crop
      removes background the network would otherwise learn to ignore, and
      re-centres the subject so the eight time bins are spatially registered
      instead of showing a hand that drifted. Isolating the two would need a
      re-centred-but-uncropped condition, which has not been run.

A4.5  PLACEMENT IS WHAT DOES THE WORK. The centre baseline has the same crop
      size, the same tensor shape and the same network, and reaches 0.366
      against the fovea's 0.731. The gap is about where the box goes and the
      fact that it moves, not about how much was kept.

A4.6  THE FRAME LENGTH AND THE RULE HAD TO BE SETTLED TOGETHER. At 50 ms the two
      fovea rules are indistinguishable and an earlier version of the notebook
      concluded the choice was free. At 10 ms they separate at 4.8 standard
      errors. Resolving them one at a time gave the wrong answer.
      Current recommended configuration: plain persistence at a 10 ms window.

A4.7  Caveats. 73 percent on eleven-class DVS128 Gesture is not competitive;
      published models reach the mid-nineties. That is intended, since the
      network is tiny, the input is 32x32 with eight bins and there is no
      augmentation, and the test set is our own held-out subjects rather than
      the official one (see A8). Only two windows were run through the
      classifier, so the optimum is somewhere in 5 to 20 ms and unlocated.

A4.9  READ THIS RESULT TOGETHER WITH A5 AND A9. Everything above is DVS128 Gesture: a
      single centred person in front of a fixed camera. Repeating the identical
      comparison on UCF-50 reversed the ordering completely. The claim that
      survives both is narrower than the one this section alone would support,
      and is stated in A5.6.

A5. DVS BENCHMARK, AND THE REVERSAL IT PRODUCED     notebook 05, section 5
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A5.1  POINTS A2 AND A5 ARE THE SAME DATASET. The paper fnins-10-00405 is Hu,
      Liu, Pfeiffer and Delbruck (2016), and the VOT Challenge 2015 event
      recordings are one of its four parts. The others are TrackingDataset,
      UCF-50 and Caltech-256.

A5.2  THE TRACKING HALF IS BENCHMARKED AND DOCUMENTED, across both VOT and
      TrackingDataset, 127 sequences. See A2. Negative, and the cause is
      understood.

A5.3  THE CLASSIFICATION HALF IS NOW RUN. UCF-50 downloaded, 17.2 GB, MD5
      verified after surviving three dropped connections. 6681 recordings across
      50 action classes on a 240x180 sensor. A 12-class, 40-clips-each subset
      was put through the identical five-way comparison from A4, at the same
      10 ms window with the same network.

A5.4  THE ORDERING REVERSES.

        condition       DVSGesture   UCF-50
        full               0.709      0.698
        persistence        0.731      0.526
        persdens           0.708      0.543
        centre             0.366      0.589
        random             0.229      0.450

        UCF-50, in standard errors of the difference
          full        - centre        +0.109    7.4 se
          centre      - persistence   +0.063    4.5 se
          persistence - random        +0.076    6.9 se

      The full frame wins, and the fovea is beaten by the trivial centre crop.
      It still clears a random box by 6.9 standard errors, so it is finding
      something, just not the right thing.

A5.5  TWO CAUSES, AND THE DATA SEPARATES THEM.
        - The task needs context. The full frame beats every crop regardless of
          where it is placed, so part of the loss is that a two-percent box
          cannot hold enough of a diving or cycling scene to identify it. That
          applies to any cropping method and is not specific to the fovea.
        - Saliency is misled by the recording. The centre crop beating the fovea
          is a separate failure, caused by the 60 Hz display refresh putting
          events across the whole frame. "Where is activity sustained" stops
          discriminating when the answer is everywhere, and the centre is a
          better prior anyway because video is shot with the subject centred.
          This is the same artifact as A2.3, doing a different kind of damage.

A5.6  WHAT THE TWO DATASETS TOGETHER LICENSE.
        Defensible: a persistence-driven fovea is a better input than the full
        sensor WHEN ACTIVITY IS SPARSE AND LOCALISED. In that regime it keeps
        the task while discarding 98 percent of the pixels and beats a fixed
        crop by a wide margin.
        Not defensible: that foveation is generally better than using the whole
        sensor. On UCF-50 it is worse than doing nothing.

A5.7  A CHEAP DIAGNOSTIC FALLS OUT OF THIS, and is probably the most portable
      thing produced this week. The ratio of what a blindly-placed box catches
      to what the fovea catches was 0.11 on DVS128 Gesture and 0.47 on UCF-50.
      Small means the scene has a clear focus of activity and the fovea has
      something to find; approaching one means activity is everywhere and there
      is nothing to select. It costs nothing and can be run on any new dataset
      before committing to the approach.

A5.8  IMPORTANT LIMIT ON HOW FAR TO READ THIS. UCF-50 is not real video, it is a
      screen recording of video. The saliency failure in A5.5 is caused by an
      artifact of how the benchmark was built, so this is NOT evidence that the
      fovea fails on real multi-object scenes, which remain untested. What it
      does establish is the boundary condition, and that A4 must always be
      quoted with A5 attached.
      It also sharpens the dataset question: whichever human-action dataset is
      chosen next must be a real recording rather than a screen capture, or it
      will reproduce this failure for reasons that say nothing about the method.

A6. CONVERTING DATASETS INTO TONIC                  tonic_convert.py
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A6.1  Tonic asks very little of a dataset: a length, indexing that returns
      (events, target), events as a structured NumPy array with fields x, y, t,
      p, and a sensor_size of (width, height, polarities). Match that and every
      transform in tonic.transforms works, along with caching and DataLoader
      collation. There is no registration step, and the download machinery can
      be skipped entirely when the data is already local.

A6.2  Built and verified: an EventDataset base implementing the contract with
      loading left abstract, plus PokerDVSCards, DVSBenchmark (walking both the
      flat VOT layout and the nested TrackingDataset one), and from_arrays as
      the general escape hatch for any new format.

A6.3  Confirmed working end to end: ToFrame, ToVoxelGrid, Denoise, Downsample,
      Compose, and torch DataLoader with tonic.collation.PadTensors.

A6.4  One trap worth knowing: targets must be numeric. PadTensors calls
      torch.tensor on the target, which fails on strings.

A6.5  It paid for itself immediately, by making the Denoise test in A2.4 a
      one-line change rather than a filter to write.

A6.6  Caveat for the supervisor: converting into Tonic is easy and worth doing
      for every new dataset, but it does not make a dataset suitable. VOT is now
      a perfectly well-formed Tonic dataset and still the wrong benchmark for
      this method. Conversion is a plumbing win, not a scientific one.

A7. CORRECTIONS MADE THIS WEEK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recorded because several changed a stated conclusion.

A7.1  "The rule choice does not matter." Reversed. It was an artifact of testing
      only at 50 ms; at 10 ms plain persistence wins at 4.8 standard errors.

A7.2  "The full condition is byte-identical between the two windows." Wrong.
      16 percent of its cells differ despite it cropping nothing, because events
      are binned by the frame they fell in, so a different window moves some
      between adjacent bins. The window touches every condition.

A7.3  "TrackingDataset is real scenes rather than video on a monitor." Wrong.
      All four parts of the benchmark were made by displaying video on a screen.
      Verified: TrackingDataset shows the same 60 Hz peak at full power.

A7.4  "A Gaussian mixture beats K-Means because it fits an ellipse." Wrong
      mechanism. A spherical mixture, which has no ellipse at all, matches full
      covariance and beats K-Means by the same margin. The advantage is soft
      assignment with free mixing weights and per-component scale.

A7.5  "Warm-starting is faster because EM needs fewer iterations." Wrong
      mechanism. Iteration counts are essentially unchanged, 11.2 against 11.7.
      Supplying explicit initial parameters makes scikit-learn skip its internal
      k-means seeding, which is where nearly all the saving comes from.

A7.6  Frame-length dwell peak. An early draft put it at 33 ms from a
      six-recording sample; a stratified eight-recording sample puts it at 75 ms.
      The ordering among 33, 50 and 75 ms is inside sampling noise and should
      not be quoted. The warm-versus-cold gap is far outside it and should.

A7.7  "The fovea keeps 2 percent of the sensor and beats using all of it." Now
      withdrawn twice over. UCF-50 reversed it on a second dataset (A5.6), and
      the official test set removed it on the original one (A9). What remains is
      that the fovea MATCHES the full sensor using 2 percent of it.

A8. HOW THE MODEL IS EVALUATED: THE TEST SET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Worth stating plainly, because it is the first thing anyone should ask about an
accuracy number.

A8.1  DVS128 GESTURE. Everything comes from the dataset's TRAINING split, which
      holds 1077 recordings from 23 people. That split was divided by person:
      17 people for training, 6 held out for testing. 802 recordings train, 275
      test. No person appears on both sides.

A8.2  Splitting by person rather than by recording is the point. All 23 people
      perform the same 11 gestures, so a random split would put the same
      person's recordings on both sides and let the network identify the person
      instead of the gesture. Accuracy would rise in every condition at once and
      the comparison the experiment exists to make would be hidden.

A8.3  THIS IS NOT THE OFFICIAL TEST SET. DVS128 Gesture ships a separate test
      archive covering six further people who appear in no training recording,
      and it was never downloaded. The protocol used here mirrors the official
      one in structure, subject-independent with six people held out, but on
      different people. Consequence: the absolute accuracies are NOT directly
      comparable to published DVS128 Gesture numbers. Every conclusion drawn is
      about the ordering between the five conditions, which is measured on one
      identical test set and is unaffected by this.

A8.4  UCF-50. 480 recordings, 12 classes, 40 clips each, spanning 25 groups.
      Split by group: 19 groups train, 6 held out. 351 train, 129 test.

A8.5  Groups matter for the same reason people do. UCF clips are named
      v_BaseballPitch_g01_c01, where g01 marks several clips cut from one source
      video. Clips from the same video share background, lighting and camera
      angle, so a random split lets the network memorise the video rather than
      learn the action.

A8.6  Repeats. Every figure is the mean over several independent training runs
      differing only in random seed: three seeds for the first pass, eight for
      the DVS128 Gesture comparisons whose ordering was in question, five for
      UCF-50. Differences are reported in standard errors of the difference so
      it is visible which orderings are real and which are noise.

A9. THE OFFICIAL TEST SET, AND THE CLAIM IT WITHDREW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A9.1  The dataset's own test archive was downloaded and put in place: 264
      recordings from subjects 24 to 29, 24 per class, none of whom appear in any
      training recording. The protocol is now train on 1077 recordings from
      subjects 1 to 23, test on those 264. This is what published DVS128 Gesture
      numbers use, so ours are comparable for the first time.

A9.2  RESULTS, five seeds, 10 ms window.

        condition      improvised   OFFICIAL
        full               0.709      0.855
        persistence        0.731      0.845
        persdens           0.708      0.812
        centre             0.366      0.477
        random             0.229      0.309

A9.3  THE HEADLINE DID NOT REPLICATE.
        improvised test set : persistence - full = +0.0223  (4.3 sigma)
        official test set   : persistence - full = -0.0100  (1.3 sigma)
      The fovea does not beat the full sensor. It matches it.

A9.4  WHAT SURVIVES, AND FIRMLY.
        persistence - centre    +0.368   45.6 sigma
        persistence - persdens  +0.033    6.3 sigma
      Placement is worth an enormous amount, and plain persistence remains the
      rule to use. Both conclusions are stronger on the official protocol than
      they were before.

A9.5  WHY THE NUMBERS MOVED. Every condition gained about 0.13, because the
      official protocol trains on all 1077 recordings rather than the 802 left
      after carving out a test set, 34 percent more data. The likely reading is
      that the fovea's apparent advantage was partly compensating for a
      data-starved network: given enough examples the network learns to ignore
      background itself. Testable by training the full condition on subsets, not
      yet done.

A9.6  THE METHODOLOGICAL LESSON, which is worth more than the number. Eight seeds
      established that the result was stable against the network's random
      initialisation, and that turned out to say nothing about whether it was
      stable against a change of evaluation data. Seed stability is not
      replication. A change-of-condition check should come before anything is
      promoted to a headline.

A10. THIS WEEK'S THREE POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A10.1 POINT 1, use VOT, relax the frame-rate constraint, focus on tracking.
      DONE, NEGATIVE. A local tracker was written (tracker.py) which fits the
      mixture only inside a search window around the previous box rather than to
      the whole frame, and is initialised once from the benchmark's own first
      annotation. Frame length was then swept freely.

        configuration                 mean IoU   prec@20px
        static box, never moves          0.119        34%
        16.7 ms  = 60 Hz refresh         0.120        28%
        33.3 ms  = 30 fps video          0.126        28%
        50 ms                            0.136        28%
        best found (alpha 0.3)           0.139        31%

      The static-box control is the finding. Every configuration lands within
      noise of a box that never moves, and loses to it on precision. Frame rate
      is not the binding constraint and relaxing it changes nothing.

A10.2 A FACTUAL POINT TO RAISE WITH HIM. There is no 60 fps footage in this
      dataset. The paper states every tracking and action recording was displayed
      at 30 fps, and the filenames say so too. The 60 Hz figure is the monitor's
      refresh rate, not the content's frame rate. Sampling at exactly one refresh
      period was tested anyway and scored 0.120 against the static box's 0.119.

A10.3 WHAT WOULD BE NEEDED. The blocker is spatial, not temporal. VOT targets
      occupy 0.2 to 8 percent of the frame while a three-component mixture over
      the whole frame produces components covering roughly a third each, which is
      the oracle IoU of 0.1 in A2.2. Local search fixes that in principle and
      still does not track, which points at the model rather than its settings.
      Matching a specific target needs an appearance model, not a density
      estimate of where events are. That is a different method and worth an
      explicit decision rather than drifting into it.

A10.4 POINT 2, evaluate against benchmark ground truth, not a classifier.
      DONE. tracker.py and evaluate.py score only against the benchmark's own
      annotations: intersection over union per frame, success at a 0.5 threshold,
      area under the success curve, centre error, and precision at 20 pixels. No
      classifier appears anywhere in that path.
      Two things came out of doing it properly.
        - The 60 Hz diagnosis was overstated, corrected in A2.3. The refresh
          modulation is 1.13 to 1.78 times the mean, not dominant, and targets
          are 1.3 to 2.5 times denser than the scene average (17.6 times for the
          small fast ball in ball1). The targets are not invisible.
        - VOT annotations are rotated quadrilaterals and we score against their
          axis-aligned bounding box, which inflates the annotated area by 1.34
          times on average and caps a perfect tracker at about 0.75 overlap,
          worse on rotated targets (gymnastics3 caps at 0.56). A real handicap,
          though nowhere near enough to explain 0.13 against 0.5.

A10.5 POINT 3, use the official test set. DONE, see A9. It withdrew the project's
      headline claim, which is the strongest argument for his having insisted on
      it.

A11. RELATIVE OVERLAP, AND THE APPEARANCE MODEL     evaluate.py, snn_tracker.py
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A11.1 THE SUPERVISOR'S POINT WAS CORRECT AND THE CEILING WAS WORSE THAN RECORDED.
      Scoring an upright fixed-area box against a rotated quadrilateral caps a
      perfect tracker below 1.0, so overlap should be divided by the achievable
      maximum. Implemented against the true quads rather than their bounding
      boxes, so it removes the axis-alignment handicap and the fixed-size one
      together. Mean achievable ceiling on VOT is 0.588, not the 0.75 recorded
      in A10.4, because freezing the box size costs more than the rotation does.

A11.2 17 OF 60 VOT SEQUENCES HAVE A CEILING BELOW 0.50, eight below 0.40. The
      standard success-at-0.5 threshold is unreachable on 28 percent of the
      benchmark for any tracker at all. TrackingDataset is much easier
      geometrically, 0.736 mean with 5 of 67 below 0.5, which accounts for part
      of the gap between the two halves.

A11.3 IT DOES NOT CHANGE THE RESULT, AND COULD NOT HAVE. The tracker and the
      static box are both upright boxes frozen at the same first-frame size, so
      they share a per-frame ceiling and dividing both by it cannot separate
      them. Static 0.136 becomes 0.237, tracker 0.081 becomes 0.143, and the
      gap widens. What changes is legibility: 14 percent of achievable against
      24 percent, rather than a bare 0.081.

A11.4 A SCORING FAULT, FIXED. 15 of 67 TrackingDataset sequences mark an absent
      or fully occluded target with NaN annotation rows, and Other/drunk2 is
      more than half absent. Those frames were scored as misses rather than
      excluded. VOT has none, so only TrackingDataset numbers were affected, and
      both methods took the same hit.

A11.5 THE TRACKING FAILURE IS NOW ISOLATED, and this is the most useful thing in
      this section. The box was assumed to be nearly stationary; it is not, it
      travels 1.02 times as far as the target. Direction coherence, the cosine
      between the box's step and the target's, says what is actually wrong:

        the target's own step-to-step coherence      0.667
        local tracker                                0.022

      The targets move smoothly and predictably and the tracker recovers three
      percent of that. The problem was never following. It is identity.

A11.6 FOUR SELECTION RULES AND A CONSTANT-VELOCITY MODEL WERE TRIED AND ALL LOST,
      over the full 60 sequences. Every one moves the box further and scores
      lower, persistence among the worst. Any rule that selects on where activity
      is concentrated fails here, because the target holds a fifth of it.

A11.7 AN APPEARANCE MODEL, BUILT AS A SPIKING NETWORK. A leaky integrate-and-fire
      neuron per pixel produces a membrane surface, which is both the standard
      event-camera time surface and the persistence integrator applied per pixel
      instead of per component, so it carries no dependence on k. The template is
      that surface inside the first annotation; matching is normalised
      cross-correlation, the normalisation being what separates it from a density
      measure; an snntorch LIF layer accumulates match evidence so the box moves
      only when a neuron spikes.

A11.8 APPEARANCE DOES CARRY IDENTITY, WHICH WAS NOT OBVIOUS.

        template source            ncc at truth   rank of truth
        the same frame                   0.969            0.8%
        the previous frame               0.796            5.6%
        frame zero                       0.033           45.8%

      A template one frame old ranks the target in the top six percent of
      candidate positions. A frame-zero template ranks it at chance, so event
      appearance goes stale within a sequence. The first row is the control that
      verifies the geometry.

A11.9 IT STILL DOES NOT TRACK, over all 60 sequences:

        configuration          raw IoU   relative    dir   wins
        static box               0.136      0.237      -   0/60
        density tracker          0.081      0.143  0.022  17/60
        snn appearance           0.084      0.141  0.064   4/60
        snn + anchor 0.3         0.078      0.126  0.028   7/60

      The appearance model matches the density tracker while nearly tripling
      direction coherence, so it is extracting real directional signal where the
      density rules extracted none. It is still 0.064 of an available 0.667, and
      a box that never moves beats both. Anchored templates and confidence-gated
      updates were tried and ruled out.

A11.10 WHY IT FAILS, PRECISELY. The 5.6 percent ranking was measured with the
      template positioned by ground truth every frame. A tracker bootstraps from
      its own estimate, so once the box slips it learns the background and then
      searches for it, and the error compounds. The information is present in the
      data; what fails is acquiring it without already knowing the answer.

A11.11 WHAT THIS LICENSES, and it is narrower and firmer than before. Density
      estimation cannot identify a target on this benchmark, evidenced at 0.667
      against 0.03. Appearance carries identity at one frame, 0.796. What is not
      solved is bootstrapping an appearance model from a tracker's own estimates,
      on a benchmark whose ceiling is 0.588 and where a third of sequences cannot
      reach the standard threshold. That is a bounded negative result rather than
      a bare one, and it is a different claim from "the method does not track".

A11.12 A FOURTH SMALL-SAMPLE INVERSION. On 10 sequences the appearance tracker
      looked clearly worse than the density tracker, 0.062 against 0.085; on 60
      it is ahead, 0.084 against 0.081. Same pattern as A5 and A9. The rule that
      partial runs here are actively misleading has now held four times.

A11.13 PIPELINE FAULTS FOUND BY AUDIT AND FIXED. Every classifier accuracy was
      the best test accuracy over 40 epochs, which selects the checkpoint on the
      test set; epoch selection now uses a validation split from the training
      subjects. The centre and random controls were sized from the persdens
      boxes although the headline comparison is persistence against centre, so

      section 6 before section 5 and referenced a deleted section 4. Notebooks 05

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A13.1 AN EXTERNAL REVIEW OF THE DRAFT REPORT FOUND THAT NOTHING ISOLATED
      PERSISTENCE. The comparison had always been fovea against fixed crop
      against random crop, which shows only that a placed crop beats an unplaced
      one. The plain mixing-weight rule was listed in the method as available and
      had never been run. It was run.

A13.2 PERSISTENCE BUYS NOTHING, on a 2x2 of instantaneous against accumulated,
      crossed with raw weight against weight per unit area:

                         raw weight   per unit area
          instantaneous     0.851         0.808
          accumulated       0.839         0.805

      size - persistence is +0.013 at 1.8 se on DVS128 Gesture and +0.022 at
      2.2 se on UCF-50: same direction on both, past the threshold on one. The
      factor that matters is raw weight against per-area, worth about 0.045.
      Accumulation, which the method is named after, is worth nothing.

A13.3 THE TITLE, THE ABSTRACT AND THE MECHANISM CLAIM ARE WITHDRAWN. Warm-
      starting survives only in the narrower form that hysteresis needs component
      identity to persist, which is true of every rule. A1.2 and B5.2 remain
      correct about identity and dwell; what is withdrawn is that any of it
      translates into task accuracy through persistence.

A13.4 UCF-50 RE-RUN UNDER THE COMMON PROTOCOL, and it is worse than A5.4
      recorded. Centre rises to 0.640, random to 0.541, persistence falls to
      0.499. Every fovea rule now loses to a randomly placed box, persistence by
      2.8 se. A5.4's "still clears a random box by 6.9 standard errors, so it is
      finding something" is false. The cause of the discrepancy was a duplicated
      reduction loop in build_crops_ucf.py that had drifted from the shared one;
      it now delegates, so the columns are identical by construction.

A13.5 THE DIAGNOSTIC SURVIVES AND n IS NOW FOUR ON EQUAL FOOTING: ratios 0.192,
      0.550, 0.592 and 0.627 against premiums +0.303, +0.059, -0.131 and -0.143.
      Monotone, all four measured the same way.

A13.6 WHAT THE PROJECT NOW CLAIMS. The method is: fit a three-component Gaussian
      mixture per frame, crop to the heaviest component. What survives is not a
      mechanism but a set of measurements about it. Placement is worth +0.316 at
      35.6 se against a matched centre crop on a localised scene. The operating
      range is roughly a quarter of the sensor to match the full one. Whether any
      of it is worth doing is predicted in advance by the blind-box ratio. This
      is a measurement paper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B5. NOTEBOOK 03, SALIENCY MAPS AND PERSISTENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B5.1  A Gaussian mixture is already a continuous density over the image plane,
      so the saliency map is the mixture itself rendered on the pixel grid:
      S(x,y) = sum over components of persistence * N(x,y | mean, covariance).
      Three variants: weighted by mixture weight, by persistence, and
      accumulated over time.

B5.2  WARM-STARTING THE MIXTURE. Fitting each frame from the previous frame's
      parameters keeps component identity, which is what makes any accumulated
      per-component measure possible. Component dwell went from 1.7 to 6.1
      frames, index switching from 60.5 to 21 percent, and it is 2.5x faster.

B5.3  Persistence defined as an accumulated quantity:
      persistence <- decay * persistence + (1 - decay) * share of this frame's
      events, with decay 0.85.

B5.4  A numerical collapse fixed with a variance floor of 1 px^2, the sensor's
      own quantisation. At the scikit-learn default a component collapsed to a
      covariance determinant of 1e-8 and produced a density spike a thousand
      times taller than any real structure.

B5.5  Fovea dwell went from 0.08 s on the original per-frame baseline to 2.97 s,
      with a longest unbroken lock of 4.28 s, about 70 percent of a recording.

STILL OPEN
  - A real multi-object scene. This is now the most important gap. The fovea
    helps when activity is sparse and localised (A5.6) and the only counter-
    example so far is a screen recording, whose failure mode is an artifact.
    Whether it helps or hurts on genuine cluttered video is untested, and
    choosing a dataset that is a real recording is the way to find out.
  - Ground truth on a real scene. Still nothing has both: the DVS benchmark has
    annotations but is a screen capture, POKER-DVS and DVS128 Gesture are real
    but unannotated.
  - The spiking classifier. Deliberately deferred so that A4 could isolate
    whether the fovea preserves information from whether an SNN trains on it.
    It now has a specific target to beat: 0.731 at a 10 ms window.
  - Isolating why the fovea beats the full frame on gestures. Background removal
    and temporal registration are both plausible and neither has been separated.
    A re-centred but uncropped condition would do it, and is a small experiment.
  - Per-pixel persistence, which would drop the dependence on k entirely.
  - Whether the saliency stage becomes spiking. The persistence update is a
    leaky integrator with a decay constant, which is what a spiking neuron's
    membrane potential is. A1 sharpens the point: a genuinely spiking front end
    would have no frame at all, and shorter frames working better is a hint in
    that direction.

SCOPE CAVEAT
  Two datasets have now been measured and they disagree, which is the single
  most important thing in this document. The fovea beats the full sensor on
  DVS128 Gesture and loses to a fixed centre crop on UCF-50. A4 must not be
  quoted without A5. The condition separating them is stated in A5.6 and can be
  checked cheaply on any new dataset using the diagnostic in A5.7.
