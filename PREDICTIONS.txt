================================================================================
PREDICTIONS REGISTERED BEFORE MEASUREMENT
================================================================================

The blind-box ratio is supposed to be a diagnostic: something you run on a new
dataset before committing to the approach, that tells you whether a fovea will
help. So far it has only ever been computed retrospectively, on the two datasets
whose outcome was already known, which is not evidence that it predicts anything.

This file exists so the predictions are on record before the measurements are
taken. Anything written above a "MEASURED" line was written first.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14 August 2026 - the diagnostic itself was wrong
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 0.11 recorded for DVS128 Gesture and the 0.47 for UCF-50 were both computed
in notebook 05 from the crops archive, in which the random control box was sized
from the persdens boxes rather than the persistence ones and was therefore about
a fifth too small in area. A smaller blind box catches fewer events and pushes
the ratio down, so both figures are biased low by an unknown amount.

Recomputed with blind_box.py, which sizes the blind box from the same rule whose
fovea it is being compared against:

    DVS128 Gesture   0.192   converged over 120 recordings (0.212, 0.202,
                             0.195, 0.192 at 90, 100, 110, 120)
                             previously recorded as 0.11

The scale therefore has to be restated, and any threshold quoted in the report
must come from the corrected tool rather than the notebook figures.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PREDICTIONS, written before the runs finished
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

UCF-50. Expect the ratio to land well above gesture's 0.192, because the
recordings are video played on a monitor and the display refresh puts events
across the whole frame. Previously recorded as 0.47 under the buggy sizing;
since the correction raised gesture's figure by about 0.08, expect UCF-50 to
land somewhere around 0.5 to 0.6. The ordering gesture < UCF-50 is the part that
matters and should be unambiguous.

DVS-LIP. Expect a HIGH ratio, comparable to or above UCF-50, and therefore a
prediction that foveation will NOT help. The reason is not that the scene is
busy but that the samples are already cropped: the DAVIS-346 recorded at
346x260 and the released data is 128x128, tightly framed on the mouth. Activity
should fill that crop, leaving a saliency mechanism nothing to select. If the
ratio comes back low instead, the diagnostic is measuring something other than
what it claims and this prediction is the evidence against it.

SL-ANIMALS-DVS. Expect a LOW ratio, comparable to or below gesture's 0.192. A
signer's hands are a small, fast, high-contrast region in an otherwise still
frame recorded from a fixed camera, which is the regime the fovea was built for.

WHY THESE TWO TOGETHER ARE WORTH THE TROUBLE. Both are real recordings rather
than screen captures, so unlike UCF-50 a failure on DVS-Lip could not be blamed
on a display artifact. If the diagnostic predicts failure on DVS-Lip and success
on SL-Animals, and training confirms both, it stops being an observation about
two datasets and becomes a tool that was tested before it was trusted. If it
gets either one wrong, that is worth more to the report than another confirmation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MEASURED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing above this line was edited after the fact.

    dataset          ratio    sd     fovea box   reading
    DVS128 Gesture   0.192   0.305     7.4%      localised, fovea has a target
    UCF-50           0.592   0.313    23.9%      spread out, fixed crop competes
    DVS-Lip          0.627   0.326    26.1%      spread out, fixed crop competes
    SL-Animals       0.550   0.349    17.4%      spread out, fixed crop competes
                             PREDICTION WRONG

UCF-50, PREDICTED 0.5 to 0.6, MEASURED 0.592. Correct, and the ordering
gesture < UCF-50 is unambiguous as required. The corrected scale is therefore
0.192 against 0.592, a wider and more usable separation than the 0.11 against
0.47 it replaces.

DVS-LIP, PREDICTED high and at or above UCF-50, MEASURED 0.627. Correct. It
converged by 10 recordings and never moved, which is far more stable than
gesture's, itself consistent with a scene that has no structure to find. The
supporting number is the fovea box covering 26.1 percent of the frame against
7.4 percent on gestures: with the crop already tight the mixture has nothing
compact to lock onto and spreads out. That is what "nothing left to foveate"
looks like in the model's own output rather than in the score.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED BY THIS. The diagnostic predicted DVS-Lip
correctly, in writing, before the run. That is one prospective success, not a
validated tool. The prediction it makes is about the CLASSIFIER OUTCOME, and
until the five-condition comparison is run on DVS-Lip and the fovea does in fact
fail to beat a fixed crop, only the intermediate quantity has been confirmed.
The classifier is the test that counts.

SL-ANIMALS, PREDICTED at or below 0.192, MEASURED 0.550. WRONG, and this is the
most useful measurement of the set.

The reader was checked first, since a bad slice would raise the ratio for
reasons having nothing to do with the scene. It is sound: tags cover 88 to 99
percent of each recording's events, none overlap, none run past the end of the
file, and 8 to 20 percent of the span is gaps between signs. The samples are
what they claim to be.

So the number is the data. Split by recording scenario it turns out not to be
one number at all:

    scenario   ratio   box%   events/frame   spread%
    indoor     0.576   18.0      324.8         56.3
    dc         0.452   16.5      157.2         53.2
    imse       0.265    9.4      241.6         55.7
    sunlight   0.248    9.9       70.2         33.2
    (gesture)  0.175    6.7      301.1         51.3

THE SAME NINETEEN SIGNS, BY THE SAME KIND OF SUBJECT, SCORE 0.248 TO 0.576
DEPENDING ONLY ON HOW THE RECORDING WAS MADE. That is a factor of 2.3 within one
dataset, and it spans the whole range between "the fovea works" and "a fixed
crop wins". The sunlight and imse recordings sit near DVS128 Gesture; the indoor
ones sit near UCF-50.

Note also that the ordering is the opposite of the obvious guess. Sunlight
recordings were expected to be noisy and score badly; they are the sparsest, at
70 events per frame against indoor's 325, and they score best.

WHAT THIS COSTS THE DIAGNOSTIC, stated plainly because it is a real limit:

  - The ratio is a property of a RECORDING CONDITION, not of a dataset or a
    task. Quoting one figure per dataset, as this project has been doing, hides
    a range wide enough to reverse the conclusion.
  - It cannot be predicted from a verbal description of a scene. "A signer's
    hands in a still frame" sounded exactly like DVS128 Gesture and is not.
    Any use of it as a planning tool has to involve running it.

WHAT IT DOES NOT COST. The diagnostic still separated the two known outcomes
cleanly and still predicted DVS-Lip correctly. And a diagnostic that only ever
confirms what was already assumed would be doing no work: this one contradicted
a confident prior, which is what a measurement is for. The honest claim is
narrower than before and better supported: run it per recording condition, and
do not guess it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE OUTCOMES, WHICH ARE WHAT THE DIAGNOSTIC ACTUALLY CLAIMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ratio was only ever an intermediate quantity. What it claims to predict is
whether a fovea beats a fixed crop of the same size on the task. All four
datasets have now been through the identical seven-condition comparison:

    dataset          ratio    persistence - centre     full - persistence
    DVS128 Gesture   0.192          +0.326                  +0.024
    SL-Animals       0.550          +0.059                  +0.260
    UCF-50           0.592          -0.063                  +0.172
    DVS-Lip          0.627          -0.143                  +0.177

MONOTONIC IN THE RATIO, ACROSS FOUR DATASETS. As the ratio rises the placement
premium falls and then changes sign. DVS-Lip is the one that counts as a
forward prediction: the ratio was measured and the failure written down here
before the classifier was run.

DVS-LIP IS THE EXTREME CASE AND WORTH QUOTING. The fovea does not merely lose
to the centre crop by 0.143 at 12.8 se; at 0.334 against a random box's 0.354
it is indistinguishable from throwing the box anywhere at all, 0.4 se. When the
data is already cropped to the subject there is nothing left for saliency to
select, and the mechanism contributes exactly nothing.

A CAVEAT ON THE ABSOLUTE NUMBERS. DVS-Lip's full condition reaches only 0.516
on 20 words against a chance of 0.05. Word-level lip reading is hard for a 71k
parameter network on 32x32 input with eight bins. Read that dataset's ordering,
not its accuracies.

A SECOND CLAIM THIS KILLED. recentred beat the full sensor on gestures by
+0.013 at 2.9 se and looked like the one route back to a positive headline. On
SL-Animals it LOSES to full by -0.112 at 11.3 se, and on DVS-Lip by -0.168 at
14.2 se. Re-centring helps only where the fovea is reliably on the subject;
where it is not, shifting the frame by it stabilises the wrong thing. The
diagnostic predicts that too, which is the useful part.

A DISTINCTION THE REPORT MUST KEEP. UCF-50 and DVS-Lip score alike but fail for
different reasons. UCF-50 is a busy scene whose display refresh spreads events
everywhere. DVS-Lip is a quiet scene already cropped so tightly that there is no
periphery left to discard. The ratio detects both, which is a point in its
favour, but "too much going on" and "nothing left to select" are not the same
condition and should not be described as one.
